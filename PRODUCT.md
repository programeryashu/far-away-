# PRODUCT

## Product
Orbit

## Purpose
Help two people who are physically far apart make the most of the moments they can share.

## Audience
People separated by distance, cities, schedules, or time zones — partners, close friends, family. Two people per session; never a crowd, never a feed.

## Core statement
Two places. One moment.

## The idea
Distance doesn't just separate people. It gives them different moments — different mornings, different midnights. Orbit finds the moments that overlap and makes them shared: one live window, one conversation, one activity, synchronized across the distance.

## What Orbit is not
- Not a social network. No profiles, no feeds, no metrics about usage.
- Not a productivity tool. The timer serves the relationship, not the other way around.
- Not an AI widget. Intelligence is embedded and quiet — a recommendation, never a chatbot.

## Voice
calm · human · precise · warm · restrained

Rules of voice:
- Say the true thing plainly ("8h 0m shared today", "one of you is asleep").
- Name people, not users or nodes.
- Never fake aliveness. Simulated content is labeled simulated.
- No exclamation marks, no buzzwords, no fabricated proof.

## The session
One person creates a connection and shares a six-character code. The other joins. While both are present everything is live and synchronized — chat, a shared timer, a shared canvas, a synchronized watch. If one drops, the other's world keeps running; on return, everything replays. Sessions are ephemeral and expire on their own.

## Design north star
Two people. Two places. Two clocks. One shared moment.
The visual signature is SHARED TIME — expressed through typography, number treatment, and comparison. Never through a glowing orb, an animated planet, or decorative 3D.
